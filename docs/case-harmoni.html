PROCESS & CONTRIBUTION

From insight to impact.

01

Discovery

Interviews, feedback, analytics, and stakeholder workshops helped clarify the experience behind the symptoms. Insights informed a supportive, actionable, personalized product direction.

02

Brand & design system

A warmer identity combined accessible typography, a calm palette, supportive language, modular components, and consistent experiences across mobile and web.

03

Design solutions

The work connected personalized onboarding, symptom tracking, AI-assisted guidance, wellness programs, educational content, and a redesigned marketing website.

04

Iteration

Feedback informed navigation, onboarding, health reports, tracking, and information clarity. The product continued to evolve through testing and collaboration.
