Harmoni® is the next evolution of ThePause®: AI-powered guidance, symptom tracking, wellness programmes and health insights for women in midlife. I first designed ThePause® with Remedy Product Studio in 2024 and continued as the product designer through the rebrand to Harmoni®.

1. Discovery

Understanding the experience behind the symptoms

Interviews, customer feedback, analytics and stakeholder workshops with the founders and healthcare experts clarified what women in perimenopause and menopause need from a health companion.

The insights pointed to a supportive, actionable and personalised direction: explain what is happening, help her track it, and turn it into a plan she can act on.

2. Brand & design system

A warmer identity, built as a system

I led the rebrand from ThePause® to Harmoni® across the mobile app and website. Accessible typography, a calm purple and lilac palette with glass surfaces, supportive language and modular components keep experiences consistent across mobile and web.

Accessibility was part of the system: contrast, readable type, touch-target sizing, clear errors and status that does not rely on colour alone.

3. Product design

From questionnaire to daily companion

The work connected personalised onboarding, symptom tracking, AI-assisted guidance, wellness programmes, educational content and a redesigned marketing website.

Feedback continued to shape navigation, onboarding, health reports, tracking and information clarity through several release cycles.

4. Funnel diagnosis

From feature guesswork to evidence

The product had too few paying users, and the team’s response was to plan more features. Without data, every new feature was a guess about a problem nobody had located.

The assumption
Onboarding was long and clinical, so women were probably dropping out before they reached the product.
What I changed
I asked the developers to instrument the journey in Mixpanel and combined it with RevenueCat subscription data. That gave the team its first end-to-end view, from first app open to trial conversion, filtered to production traffic only.
What the data showed
Onboarding was holding up — almost everyone who started it finished. The real drop-offs were earlier and later: the email one-time-code sign-up step, and the paywall at the end of onboarding.
Why it mattered
The priority moved from building new features to fixing two specific moments of trust.

5. Trust-led redesign

Fixing the two moments of trust

Registration asked for an email code before a woman had seen a privacy policy — a lot to ask from someone about to share health data. The paywall had been built outside the design process and listed features rather than the value of her plan.

I redesigned the path from the first promise to renewal on the existing purple and lilac glass design system, without introducing new visual patterns.

Kept promise, then price

Onboarding now tells her what she will learn about herself before asking anything clinical, and hands that result over before any mention of price. The paywall becomes the conclusion of her story, not a cold ask.

REDESIGN CHANGES

  • Privacy information before registration
  • Apple and Google sign-in alongside the email code
  • A promise screen before the questionnaire, and a payoff screen before the paywall
  • Paywall rebuilt around her plan, with trust signals at the decision point
  • Four trust-signal framings prepared for A/B testing
  • A value recap before the trial ends, not a warning
  • A home screen that carries her priorities forward

6. Measure next

A plan the team can measure

I presented the findings to the founders as a ship, test and instrument plan, together with a competitive review of pricing and value propositions across six menopause and women’s health apps.

The analysis also mapped what the team could not yet see — store impressions and downloads, sandbox purchases mixed into subscription events — and defined the access and tracking needed to close those gaps.

The redesigned sign-up and paywall are prepared for A/B testing; their impact has not yet been measured.

SHIP · TEST · INSTRUMENT

  • Ship: paywall, trial-ending recap and home screen redesign
  • Test: trust-signal framings against the redesigned paywall
  • Instrument: App Store Connect, Play Console and RevenueCat gaps
  • Locate the exact break in the email-code sign-up

A new identity and design system, a set of personalised health experiences, and a funnel analysis that changed what the team works on next.
