03 / DESIGN DECISIONS

Every choice serves the person evaluating me.

  • A CV button on mobile onlyOn desktop the CV is always in the top navigation. On a phone the navigation folds into a menu — and portfolio links are often opened from LinkedIn or email on a phone. So the mobile hero puts CV right next to “Get in touch”: one tap instead of a menu hunt. Desktop keeps just two hero actions, because the CV is already one click away.
  • Each case in its product’s own styleA case page takes on its product’s brand colours, so you see the product as it really looked, not re-skinned into my palette. Navigation, typography and spacing stay constant — the same idea as a themeable design system: one structure, many brands. It also shows range across very different products at a glance.
  • “See my work” before “Let’s talk”The main hero action leads to the cases, not the contact form: a recruiter needs proof before they reach out.
  • Covers that preview the productEach project card shows the product’s logo and real screens; on hover it reveals the brand colours. You know what a case is about before you click.
  • The menu never disappearsCase studies are long, so the header stays pinned with a soft blur — CV and Contact are always one click away, even mid-case.
  • Client work first, experiments separateProfessional cases lead; independent products live in their own section, so commercial impact and personal experiments are never confused.
  • Lists over long proseLong project narratives are broken into scannable rows and panels, because reviewers skim before they read.
  • Light and dark themesThe site respects the visitor’s system setting and remembers their choice.
  • Motion with an off switchThe animated hero word and hover effects add life, and turn off for anyone who has reduced motion enabled.

04 / DESIGN TO DELIVERY

AI-assisted. Personally owned.

  • ClaudeMy main build partner: implementing pages and components, refactoring, and reviewing code and copy.
  • FigmaLayouts, components, case visuals and device mockups. Claude reads my Figma files directly through the Figma connection, so case content moves from design to page without retyping.
  • Nano Banana & HiggsfieldAI-generated imagery — covers, illustrations and visual scenes — art-directed and edited to fit each case.
  • Cursor & ChatGPTSecond opinions on code and wording.
  • GitHubEvery change goes through a branch and pull request before it’s published on GitHub Pages — more than 280 commits and 65 merged pull requests since July 2026.
  • PerformanceImages converted to WebP with a small Node script: total image weight dropped from 62 MB to 11 MB.

AI speeds up the making; the decisions, the review and the responsibility for the result are mine.

The implementation includes semantic page structure, visible keyboard focus, responsive layouts, theme controls and reduced-motion alternatives. These are implementation choices, not a claim of independently certified WCAG compliance.
