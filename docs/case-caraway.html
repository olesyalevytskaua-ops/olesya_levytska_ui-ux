Caraway Health

The care you need, all in one place.

A healthcare platform for Gen Z connecting mental, physical, and reproductive health support. The design work spans a mobile member experience and a web dashboard for medical professionals.

PROCESS & CONTRIBUTION

From insight to impact.

01

Mission & users

The experience was shaped around young women transitioning to college and navigating new health and support needs.

02

Research

Competitive analysis, personas, journey mapping, card sorting, brainstorming, and feature prioritization informed product structure and usage patterns.

03

Wireframing & design system

Wireframes aligned stakeholders around information hierarchy, project flows, and key features. A shared system supported visual consistency across the app and clinician dashboard.

04

Resulting solution

The member experience connects daily health tasks, tracking, articles, and medical conversations. The clinician dashboard supports patient information and communication as the product evolves.
