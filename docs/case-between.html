Between Health

Connecting mothers and doulas.

A platform connecting expecting and postpartum mothers with doulas to support a positive birth experience. The design focused on prioritising a realistic MVP and building a mobile-first foundation.

Role
UI/UX Designer
Platforms
Mobile-first responsive web app
  • Product discovery
  • MVP prioritisation
  • User flows
  • Component libraries

PROCESS & CONTRIBUTION

From insight to impact.

01

Discovery

Requirements gathering, user stories, competitor research, mind maps, and MoSCoW prioritisation helped narrow the initial scope. The research indicated that more than 80% of users would access the platform from mobile devices.

02

Brand guidelines

A shared style guide established a calm green palette, typography, and reusable components.

03

Design solutions

The MVP connected registration for mothers and doulas, profile management, calendars, payment information, doula discovery, reviews, and contracts.

04

Delivery

Mobile and desktop designs, interactive prototypes, and developer collaboration supported a consistent responsive experience.
