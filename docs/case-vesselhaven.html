PROCESS & CONTRIBUTION

From insight to impact.

01

Discovery

Research mapped a complex ecosystem of roles, operational tasks, and communication needs. Interviews, journey mapping, and feature prioritization informed the product structure.

02

UX design

The information architecture connected dashboards, role-based workflows, vessel maintenance, crew tasks, profiles, bookings, and community experiences.

03

Design system & prototyping

A flexible system of colors, typography, status indicators, forms, navigation, and reusable components supported interactive prototypes and developer handoff.

04

Testing & product experience

Testing with captains and owners informed navigation, dashboards, role clarity, bookings, and task management. The final experience connected operations with a marketplace and community.
