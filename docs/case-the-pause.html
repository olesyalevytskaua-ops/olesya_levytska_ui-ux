ThePause

A supportive everyday health companion.

An AI-powered menopause companion designed around health data collection, daily routines, symptom tracking, community, and guided support. This case study documents the earlier product before Harmoni.

PROCESS & CONTRIBUTION

From insight to impact.

01

Discovery

Requirements, user stories, research, competitor analysis, and prioritisation shaped a focused MVP with daily health tasks, tracking, recommendations, and community.

02

Brand guidelines

A purple and pink visual language connected branding, typography, colours, and reusable interface components.

03

Design solutions

Responsive screens and interactive prototypes connected daily tasks, questionnaires, insights, and supportive interactions. Collaboration with a medical expert helped refine data collection.

04

Work with feedback

Testing helped identify useful features and areas requiring iteration. The CV reports that interactive prototypes improved task accuracy by 30%.
