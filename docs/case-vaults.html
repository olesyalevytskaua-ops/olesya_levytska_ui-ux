Self-storage · Booking platform

Vaults1

A clearer journey from search to reservation.

A premium self-storage platform that helps customers search, compare, and reserve secure units online. The experience connects marketing pages, a pricing calculator, booking, payment, and customer account functionality.

PROCESS & CONTRIBUTION

From insight to impact.

01

Discovery

Customer journey mapping, competitor analysis, information architecture, and responsive planning identified opportunities to make storage choices and pricing easier to understand.

02

UX design process

The journey supports users from the first visit through selection, pricing, reservation, payment, and confirmation. Iterative design focused on clear decisions and fewer points of friction.

03

UI design

A modular visual system connected booking components, forms, pricing tables, interactive cards, checkout, and CMS templates.

04

Final solution

The result is a cohesive multi-step reservation experience with consistent marketing and transactional pages, designed to support future business growth.
