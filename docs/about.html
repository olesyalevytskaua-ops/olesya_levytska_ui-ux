ABOUT / HOW I GOT HERE

From numbers
to products.

I’m Olesya, a Senior Product & UX/UI Designer. I came to design through ten years of business analysis, and I still start with the question: what is really going on here?

My story

Analyst first, designer next

For a decade I analysed business processes, organised workflows and set up accounting and office software. That work taught me to read data and to find where a process breaks before anyone builds a fix.

I moved into UX through project management, then trained in UI/UX design in 2021. Since then I have designed healthcare, SaaS and booking products, built design systems that developers actually use, and learned to ship alongside engineers in Git and code.

How I work

Evidence first, then design

I like products where the problem isn’t obvious. I would rather spend a week finding the real break in a funnel than a month designing features nobody asked for.

IN PRACTICE

  • Research, analytics and prototypes before heavy build
  • Design systems with tokens in code, not just in Figma
  • Handoff specs that cover every state, so developers don’t guess
  • AI for exploring and prototyping, with my own review of every output
  • Accessibility as the floor: WCAG AA and real tap targets

Beyond the day job

Learning in public

I write UX, Explained Simply, a carousel series that explains accessibility, analytics and product decisions without jargon. I also build small products of my own, like the midlife crisis quiz.

Away from the screen, I’m usually outdoors: hiking in the mountains, paddleboarding in summer and skiing in winter.
